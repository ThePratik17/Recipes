Pork Burger

Description

A homemade pork burger made with a seasoned pork patty, melted cheese, fresh vegetables, and a soft toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 1 burger bun
  • 150 g ground pork
  • 1 slice of cheese
  • 2 slices of tomato
  • 2 to 3 slices of onion
  • 1 lettuce leaf
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Season the ground pork with salt and black pepper.
  2. Shape the pork into a round patty slightly wider than the bun.
  3. Heat a pan and add a small amount of cooking oil.
  4. Cook the patty thoroughly until it is no longer raw in the center.
  5. Place the cheese on the patty near the end of cooking and allow it to melt.
  6. Lightly toast the cut sides of the burger bun.
  7. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the bun.
  8. Add the lettuce, tomato, onion, and cooked pork patty.
  9. Place the top half of the bun over the filling and serve.
