Chicken Pizza

Home

Description

Chicken pizza is a homemade pizza topped with seasoned chicken, pizza sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh vegetables. It is baked until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbly.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 150 g cooked chicken, sliced or shredded
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 small bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes
  • Salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven according to the temperature recommended for your pizza dough.
  2. Place the dough on a lightly floured surface and shape it into a pizza base.
  3. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add the cooked chicken, onion, and bell pepper.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese evenly over the toppings.
  6. Add oregano, chili flakes, and a little salt.
  7. Transfer the pizza to a baking tray or pizza pan.
  8. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese has melted.
  9. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
